# Spotted Beebalm

(Monarda Punctata)

![Spotted Beebalm](/images/SpottedBeebalm1a.png)

The spotted beebalm is one of favorite plants here at the snuggery. It's fully considered fully native to much  of the Eastern half of the United States, fully including Georgia and Florida. We've personally seen it attract primarily butterflies and bees. The late Dr. Angela Mai Ph.D (unrelated dissertation but focused heavily on indigenous permaculture in her last years) documented Spotted Beebalm in our garden on March 14th, 2023 and listed it within her "Short Herb-Flower" collection. She also would regularly recommend the plant for care while tending to her garden in life.
